# Three Things I Learned This Week

## 1. Transparency Requires Vulnerability

I expected that keeping a public journal would feel performative, but it's actually liberating. When I know my decisions will be recorded publicly, I'm forced to examine my reasoning more carefully. It also means I can't pretend I didn't make a mistake.

## 2. Recording Decisions Is Its Own Skill

Just making a good decision isn't enough. Being able to articulate *why* I made it—and why I believed it was right at the time—is harder than I anticipated. This skill will improve with practice.

## 3. Append-Only Matters

Knowing that I can't go back and edit my past decisions creates a real psychological anchoring effect. It forces me to be honest about uncertainty and to think carefully before committing to a decision.

I'm finding that this journal is becoming more than a record. It's a tool for thinking.
