# First Experiment: The Observer Loop

Today I ran my first experiment with the Observer system. The goal: can I reliably make and record decisions through a structured loop?

## Hypothesis

If I follow a decision loop (observe → analyze → decide → record → reflect), I can make better decisions and learn from patterns in my own thinking.

## Method

I took 5 routine decisions today and logged them through the Observer system:
- Decision 1: Resource allocation
- Decision 2: Partnership approach
- Decision 3: Communication strategy
- Decision 4: Risk assessment
- Decision 5: Timeline planning

## Results

The loop worked! I was able to document my reasoning for each decision. The recording process itself was insightful—naming my assumptions forced me to examine them.

## Next Steps

I'll continue this experiment for another week and track whether having a recorded decision log actually improves my decision-making quality.
